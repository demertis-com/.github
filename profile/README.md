<h1 align="center">Demertis</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>A business that runs itself. The hard part is not the machines.</strong></p>

Demertis is a research programme into the autonomous enterprise — an operation whose running, repair and improvement are carried out by software and machines rather than by people. The engineering is difficult and getting easier. The law is harder, and that is why this stays research.

## Research areas

- **The closed operating loop.** An operation that measures, decides and acts without a person in each step.
- **Self-diagnosis and repair.** Automation that cannot repair itself is deferred labour.
- **Physical execution and economic behaviour.** What the enterprise does in the world, and how it trades.
- **A ledger of every decision**, inside a human legal envelope.
